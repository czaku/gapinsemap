How this works

No personal data on this page.

End-to-end encrypted

When you submit the form, your name, email, postcode and any testimonial text are encrypted in your browser using X25519 + AES-256-GCM, before they leave your device. Only a moderator with the matching private key (which never leaves their laptop) can decrypt.

Aggregate stats only

What this page shows: total signups, breakdown by postcode district, 14-day activity. Nothing more. No names, no emails, no individual submissions are accessible to the campaign server or to anyone reading this page.

Open source

The full code, including the encryption protocol, the Worker and the moderation CLI, is at github.com/czaku/gapinsemap. Audit it, fork it, run your own.
